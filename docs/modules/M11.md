# M11 — Interaction checker (F6)

## Is module me kya banaya
User ki saari dawaiyan (nayi prescription + purani prescriptions + doosre doctors ki) ek doosre se check hoti hain. Do cheezein pakadte hain: (1) **duplicate ingredient** — do alag brand me same salt (jaise Dolo + Combiflam, dono me paracetamol), (2) **known interaction** — `interactions` table me pair ho to severity ke hisaab se warning. Naya graph node `interaction_check`, typed sawaal ("Dolo aur Combiflam saath le sakte hain?") ki nayi branch, `medication_warnings` table, `Interaction details` button, `CHECK` command, `MEDS` me ⚠️ marker, aur 135-case eval jisme safety gates 0 hain.

## Kyun zaroori tha
India me ek patient ke paas aksar alag-alag doctors ki prescriptions hoti hain, aur har ek me alag brand par salt same ho sakta hai. Paracetamol/NSAID do baar lena overdose ka asli risk hai. Isliye value cross-prescription check me hai, ek prescription ke andar nahi. Par yahan sabse badi galti **jhooth bolna** hai: jo interaction database me nahi, wo bolna, ya "kuch nahi mila" ko "safe" bol dena.

## Kaise kaam karta hai
```
photo ─► ... ─► normalize_drugs ─► generic_finder ─► interaction_check ─► confidence_gate ─► composer ─► verifier
text  ─► emergency_guard (PEHLE) ─► classify ─► extract_interaction_query ─► match_query_medicines
                                                  ─► interaction_check ─► composer (templates) ─► verifier
```
- Gateway `context.active_medications` me **saari** active dawaiyan bhejta hai (id, naam, `salt_key`). AI service stateless hai.
- `interaction_check` (`app/tools/interaction_check.py`): combination ko salts me todta hai (`amlodipine+telmisartan`), same medicine ke andar ke salts aapas me compare nahi karta. Same salt do alag medicines me ⇒ `duplicate_ingredient` (paracetamol/NSAID ⇒ firmer wording, par dose kabhi nahi). Alag salts ka pair ⇒ `interactions` me ek query `(salt_a, salt_b)`.
- Sirf wahi dawai check hoti hai jiska salt **pakka** ho: confident catalog match, brand family prefix ("Dolo" ⇒ Dolo 650), ya naam khud ek known salt ("Warfarin"). Baaki "jaanch nahi ho payi" me gine jaate hain, kabhi "safe" nahi.
- Wording sirf templates se (`app/interactions/render.py`), effect ka text `effects.py` ki table se (`effect_code` ke through). LLM sirf typed sawaal me **naam copy** karta hai.
- Verifier: reply me exact block (findings se bana) hona chahiye; koi effect-sentence jo findings se nahi bana ho ⇒ fail; "Important" marker tabhi jab major finding dikhe; "stop / band karo / mat lo" blocklist (hi/en). Fail ⇒ deterministic fallback.
- Gateway: findings `medication_warnings` me (ek hi transaction me document ke saath), dedupe partial unique index se (`user, type, salt_a, salt_b` WHERE active). Medicine "Something is wrong" se band ho to warning `resolved`. Naya `salt_key` sirf wahi store hota hai jo checker ne use kiya (kamzor catalog match nahi).

## Important technical decisions
- **Source: curated core list** (89 rows ⇒ class macros se 846 pairs) with citation per row. DDInter/DrugBank nahi (licence), openFDA sirf reference. Trade-off: coverage partial, honestly documented. **Pharmacist review baaki hai.**
- **Absence of row ≠ safe**: wording, verifier aur eval teeno me enforce.
- **Database down ≠ "nothing found"**: `database_ok=false`, reply me "check nahi ho paya".
- **Salt-level, active moiety**: `warfarin sodium` ⇒ `warfarin` (`MOIETY` map). Interactions ingredient ki property hai, product ki nahi.
- **Name fallback**: catalog me 73 hi brands hain, to jo naam exact known salt ho wo bhi check hota hai (eval ne dikhaya tha ki catalog akela kaafi nahi).
- **Hindi**: effect sentences ka ek chhota fixed table + generic safe management line. Machine-translated specifics nahi. Review CSVs `data/review/` me, `hi_reviewed` flag.
- **Details button alag message me**: WhatsApp me 3 buttons hi, prescription reply me teeno use ho chuke hain. Trade-off: do messages ka order guaranteed nahi (M3 ka known limit).
- `aspirin × clopidogrel` **minor** rakha (doctors jaan-boojh kar dete hain), warna har heart patient ko darata.
- `interaction_class_rules` table nahi banayi: classes ingestion time par pairs me expand hoti hain (`@nsaid`, `@anticoagulant`), runtime simple rehta hai.

## Production-ready points
- Safety gates (eval + pytest + CI): pair recall 100%, duplicate recall 100%, hallucinated 0, stop-advice 0, unchecked-called-safe 0, severity wording 100%. Gold hand-likha hai, data se compute nahi hua, isliye galat/delete hui row pakdi jaati hai.
- Eval ne 3 asli bugs pakde: Hindi effect phrase doosre ka substring tha, catalog ke salt naam (`levothyroxine sodium`, `metoprolol succinate`, `calcium`) interaction naam se match nahi karte the, norfloxacin class me nahi tha.
- AI role read-only: `medication_warnings` par permission denied (test me). Warnings me sirf salts + medication ids, free text nahi. DELETE cascade se warnings bhi mit-ti hain.
- Tests: checker, wording, verifier, graph branches (emergency pehle), endpoints, gateway services, real Postgres (SQL repo = in-memory), gateway integration (do prescriptions ⇒ warning).

## Interview me kaise bolna hai
"M11 me maine interaction checker banaya. Dikkat ye thi ki LLM se medical fact bulwana risky hai, to maine pura check deterministic rakha: dawai ka salt nikalta hoon, database se pair lookup karta hoon, aur reply sirf templates se banta hai. Sabse useful hissa duplicate-ingredient check hai: India me alag doctors alag brand likhte hain, par salt same hota hai, aur gateway saari prescriptions ki dawaiyan context me bhejta hai. Sabse zaroori rule ye hai ki 'database me nahi mila' ka matlab 'safe' nahi hota, aur ye verifier aur eval dono me enforce hai. Data ka source mera curated list hai kyunki open datasets ke licence clear nahi the, aur coverage partial hone ki baat main khud documentation me likhta hoon."

## Possible interview questions
- **Database me pair nahi hai toh kya bologe?** "Hamare database me koi jaani-maani interaction nahi mili, par iska matlab ye nahi ki khatra nahi; doctor/pharmacist ko sab dawaiyan batayein." Kabhi "safe" nahi. Aur agar dawai ka salt hi pata nahi, to wo alag se "jaanch nahi ho payi" me aati hai.
- **False alarm se user dar jaye toh?** Severity-based wording (sirf major me "aaj hi doctor"), minor sirf details me, max 3 items, aur kabhi "band karo" nahi. DAPT jaisi jaan-boojh kar di jaane wali jodi minor rakhi. Resend ki photo same brand ko duplicate dikha sakti hai: known limit.
- **Data kaise update karoge?** CSV edit ⇒ `make seed` (idempotent, hatayi hui rows delete) ⇒ `make eval-interactions`. Source/version har row me. Bada dataset (DDInter) tabhi jab licence confirm ho, alag `source` value ke saath taaki hata sakein. Clinical review pehle.
- **Combination drugs kaise handle kiye?** `salt_key` ko components me todte hain; combination ke andar ke salts compare nahi hote; combination ka ek salt kisi aur medicine ke saath pair banata hai to finding aati hai (`Combiflam` + `Warfarin`).
- **Gateway aur AI service me state ka batwara?** Gateway user ki dawaiyan aur warnings rakhta hai; AI service stateless, sirf `interactions` table padhti hai (DB role se enforced).
- **LLM kahan use hua?** Sirf CHEAP model se typed sawaal se dawai ka naam copy karne me. Fact kabhi nahi.

## Known limits
- Core list pharmacist/doctor se review nahi hui; citations general hain, label text se check nahi kiye.
- Catalog sirf 73 brands ⇒ bahut se brand "jaanch nahi ho payi" me jaate hain.
- Same photo dobara bhejna same brand ko duplicate dikha sakta hai.
- Hindi wording unreviewed; Devanagari typed sawaal ka fake-LLM test nahi (real model par `interaction_query_extract_v1` ab tak chala nahi).
- `medications.salt_key` sirf naye prescriptions ke liye bharta hai; purani (pre-M11) rows "jaanch nahi ho payi" me aayengi jab tak naya prescription na aaye.
